How Goldcast Scores the Sky ↓

Goldcast analyses six atmospheric variables from real-time weather model data. Each is weighted by how much it empirically affects the visual quality of a sunrise or sunset. The final score is 0–100, representing predicted photographic quality.

High Cloud Cover
Cirrus clouds at altitude are the single biggest contributor to a dramatic sky. They act as a canvas, catching and scattering warm light across a wide area. Ideal: 20–80%. Too little = featureless sky. Too much = total block.
Low Cloud at the Horizon
The variable most likely to ruin an otherwise promising sunset. A bank of low cloud near the horizon blocks the sun at the exact moment it produces the warmest light. Even moderate coverage significantly reduces the score.
Humidity
Low humidity means cleaner air and more saturated, punchy colors. High humidity scatters light and washes out tones — though it can produce beautiful soft haze. Extreme humidity above 85% is penalised heavily.
Precipitation
Active rainfall is a near-certain kill for color. The hour immediately after a storm clears is often the most dramatic light of the year — post-storm air is clean with residual cloud texture at altitude.
Visibility
Reduced visibility from haze adds atmospheric depth and can be photogenic. Extremely low visibility under 5km typically indicates heavy fog or pollution dense enough to block light entirely.
Wind Speed
Moderate wind is a slight positive — it creates cloud movement and texture. Very high winds introduce dust or turbulence that reduces color saturation and image sharpness.
75–100 — Epic Light. Rare. Multiple factors aligned. Worth rearranging your day for.
55–74 — Great Conditions. Strong showing expected. Go if you can.
35–54 — Worth Shooting. Modest but workable. Choose your subject carefully.
0–34 — Flat Light. Uninspiring sky expected. Best for subjects that don't need dramatic sky.

All forecasts rely on Open-Meteo weather model data and are inherently probabilistic. Conditions can change rapidly. Forecasts refresh hourly — scores will shift as the event approaches. Goldcast is a planning tool, not a guarantee.
