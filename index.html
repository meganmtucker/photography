About Me

My name is Megan Tucker, and I am a Massachusetts-based photographer. I am mostly self-taught and love shooting a variety of subjects. My favorite shots are captured outdoors while hiking or traveling, but I also enjoy shooting portraits, events, and real estate photography.

I started shooting when I was 13 and got my first point-and-shoot camera. Since then, my gear and expertise have evolved. As of 2023, I am a certified FAA Part 107 pilot, allowing me to capture drone photos commercially.

I am also the creator of LightCast Suite, a free photography conditions platform. LightCast provides forecasts for upcoming golden hour light, milky way visibility, and drone flying conditons to help outdoor photographers across 93+ countries chase better light.

I currently shoot portraits, events, and real estate photos for hire. I also commission prints of my photos. If you have any questions or want to book a shoot, get in touch!

My portfolio contains my best and favorite work, spanning several years. Check out my Instagram for up-to-date content!
