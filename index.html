About Me

Welcome to my photo portfolio! My name is Megan Tucker, and I am a Massachusetts-based photographer. I am mostly self-taught and love shooting a variety of subjects. My favorite shots are captured outdoors while hiking or traveling but I also love shooting portraits, events, and real estate.

I started shooting when I was 13 and got my first point-and-shoot camera. Since then, my gear and expertise has evolved. As of 2023, I am a certified FAA Part 107 pilot, allowing me to capture drone photos commercially.

I currently shoot portraits, events, and real estate photos for hire. I also commission prints of my photos. If you have any questions or want to book a shoot, fill out the form below to get in touch!
